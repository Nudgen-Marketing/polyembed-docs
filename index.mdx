PolyEmbed is a widget for publishers. You add one embed snippet to your article template, and every story shows the live [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com) prediction market that best matches its headline. Readers can follow the odds, or connect a wallet and trade inside the article. You earn a share of the builder fees on trades placed from your pages.

## What you get

- **No market picking.** The widget searches Polymarket with each article's title and keywords on every page load, and ranks results by relevance, liquidity and freshness.
- **No empty boxes.** When no relevant market is live, the widget collapses to zero height.
- **Trading in the article.** Readers sign in with email or their own wallet, fund a Polymarket deposit wallet, and buy or sell outcome shares. Polymarket's relayer covers gas.
- **Per-article analytics.** Impressions, Polymarket clicks, interaction rate and widget errors for every widget.
- **Revenue share.** You keep 70% of the builder fees on trades from your pages, paid Net-30.
