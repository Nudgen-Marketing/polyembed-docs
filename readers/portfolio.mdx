The PolyEmbed portfolio shows your Polymarket positions, whether you bought them from a PolyEmbed widget or on Polymarket.

## Open your portfolio

Go to [polyembed.io/portfolio](https://polyembed.io/portfolio) and either:

- Sign in with email or connect your browser wallet, or
- Enter any wallet address (`0x` followed by 40 hex characters) to view it read-only.

Every portfolio has a shareable address: `https://polyembed.io/portfolio/0x…`. Click the copy link control on the profile card to share it.

## What it shows

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Wallet address and headline P&L |
| **P&L chart** | Profit and loss over time |
| **Positions** | Open positions with current value. Resolved winners are marked **Ready to redeem** |
| **Closed** | Resolved and sold positions with realized P&L |
| **Open orders** | Orders still resting on the book. You can cancel your own when signed in |
| **Activity** | Trades, redemptions and rewards as they happen |

Use the search box to filter positions by market.

## Redeeming winnings

When a market resolves in your favor, the position shows **Ready to redeem**. Redeem it on [polymarket.com](https://polymarket.com) to turn winning shares into pUSD.

<Note>
  Portfolio data comes from Polymarket's public data. Anyone with a wallet address can view its portfolio, the same as on Polymarket.
</Note>
