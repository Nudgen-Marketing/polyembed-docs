## How it works

The affiliate program is for people who introduce publishers to PolyEmbed, such as agencies, newsletter operators and publisher consultants. You earn **10% of PolyEmbed's net revenue** from every publisher you refer, for as long as that publisher keeps PolyEmbed live.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Book a 30-minute call">
    [Book a call](https://cal.com/toannhu/30min) and tell us who you work with. We walk through the widget, the revenue split and which publishers fit best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Introduce your publishers">
    We set up your referral record and share a demo site, setup guide and talking points. Publishers go live with one [HTML snippet](/install/html-embed) or the [WordPress shortcode](/install/wordpress).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid monthly">
    As readers trade from your publishers' widgets, PolyEmbed earns builder fees. Your share is paid monthly from reconciled revenue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the 10% is paid on

Builder fees on trades placed from a referred publisher's widgets are split first with the publisher. The publisher keeps 70% (see [Earnings and payouts](/publishers/earnings)), and PolyEmbed keeps 30%. You receive 10% of PolyEmbed's 30%.

| For every $1,000 in builder fees | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Publisher's share (70%) | $700 |
| PolyEmbed's net revenue (30%) | $300 |
| **Your share (10% of PolyEmbed's net)** | **$30** |

Only fees PolyEmbed actually receives count. Revenue is reconciled against Polymarket's builder-trades feed, the same way publisher earnings are.

Read the full [rev-share and payout policy](https://polyembed.io/policies/rev-share).

## Attribution

We record each publisher you introduce against your referral record before they sign up. If two affiliates introduce the same publisher, the first recorded introduction wins.

Referrals are recorded manually after your call. There is no referral link or tracking dashboard.

## Rules

- **No self-referrals.** If you run a publication yourself, [sign up as a publisher](/publishers/account) and keep the publisher revenue share instead.
- **No prohibited claims.** Never promise returns, give trading advice or imply that Polymarket endorses you or PolyEmbed. Follow the [prohibited claims policy](https://polyembed.io/policies/prohibited-claims).

<Warning>
  Commissions earned through prohibited claims, self-referrals or abuse can be withheld.
</Warning>
