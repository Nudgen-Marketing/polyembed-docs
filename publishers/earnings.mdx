## How you earn

Every order placed from a PolyEmbed widget carries PolyEmbed's registered Polymarket builder code. Polymarket charges builder fees on those trades. You keep **70% of the builder fees** on trades placed from your pages.

Only fees PolyEmbed actually receives count. Revenue is reconciled against Polymarket's builder-trades feed, and trades that can't be matched to a confirmed builder trade don't earn.

## Payout terms

| Term | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue share | 70% of reconciled builder fees |
| Schedule | Net-30, after each period closes |
| Minimum payout | $100 per period |
| Method | Manual, after review |

Payouts may be held for abuse review, for example for self-trading, wash trading or traffic that breaks the [Publisher Terms](https://polyembed.io/policies/publisher-terms). Read the full [rev-share and payout policy](https://polyembed.io/policies/rev-share).

## The Earnings page

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Lifetime earned** | Total across all periods |
| **In payout** | Amounts requested or approved and not yet paid |
| Periods table | Each period's amount and status |

Periods appear once readers trade from your widgets.

## Payout statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `requested` | The period is waiting for review |
| `approved` | Approved and scheduled for payment |
| `paid` | Sent |
| `rejected` | Not paid, for example because it failed review. Contact support for details |

<Info>
  Accept the payout terms in **Agreements** before your first payout. Requests can't be made until you do.
</Info>

## Getting paid faster

- Accept all three agreements early.
- Keep the widget on pages where it's relevant. Readers trade on markets that match what they're reading.
- Email [contact@polyembed.io](mailto:contact@polyembed.io) to set up or change payout details.
