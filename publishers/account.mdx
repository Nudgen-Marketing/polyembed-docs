## Sign in

Go to [polyembed.io/publisher-login](https://polyembed.io/publisher-login). You can:

- **Continue with Google**, or
- **Enter your email**, click **Email me a sign-in link**, and open the link PolyEmbed sends you.

There is no separate sign-up form. Your publisher account is created the first time you sign in. The old `/login` and `/signup` addresses redirect to the same page.

<Note>
  Open the email sign-in link in the same browser you requested it from. If you open it somewhere else, the dashboard asks you to enter your email address again before signing you in.
</Note>

## The dashboard

| Section | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Launch checklist while you set up, then headline numbers once a widget is live |
| **Domains** | Add domains and verify that you own them |
| **Widgets** | Create widgets and copy their embed code |
| **Analytics** | Impressions, Polymarket clicks, interaction rate and widget errors |
| **Earnings** | Revenue-share periods and payout status |
| **Agreements** | Accept the publisher, trading-risk and payout terms |

The **Overview** page shows a four-step checklist: accept the agreements, verify a domain, create a widget, and paste the snippet. Each step links to where you complete it.

## Accept the agreements

Open **Agreements** and accept each one. Each agreement unlocks a part of the product:

| Agreement | Covers | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Publisher Terms](https://polyembed.io/policies/publisher-terms) | Domain ownership, approved copy, analytics and suspension rules | Domain verification |
| [Trading risk disclosure](https://polyembed.io/policies/trading-risk) | Prediction markets carry risk; trading depends on Polymarket availability | In-article trading |
| [Rev-share and payout policy](https://polyembed.io/policies/rev-share) | Net-30 payouts reconciled against builder revenue | Payout requests |

You can add domains before accepting the Publisher Terms, but you can't run verification until you do.

## Account status

PolyEmbed can suspend a publisher account, a domain or a widget after an abuse review. A suspended widget, a widget on a suspended domain, or any widget of a suspended publisher stops rendering and collapses on your pages. Email [contact@polyembed.io](mailto:contact@polyembed.io) if you believe a suspension is a mistake.
